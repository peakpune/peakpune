How to host your first thing

01

Keep it small

One plan, one place, one time. A Sunday morning run or a chai meetup is enough.

02

Say it clearly

Where, when, what it costs, who it is for. If people have to ask, they will not.

03

Welcome the new person

Someone will come alone. Say hi first. That is the difference between a one-off and a community.

04

Do it again

The second time is when it starts to feel like a thing.

Women and everyone else: you are wanted here

A city is more fun when more kinds of people host it. Women, men, queer folks, students, new arrivals, people of every age, language and background are welcome to host, volunteer and show up. If you run a women's community, a safe space or a group for people who are usually left out of the plan, send it in.
