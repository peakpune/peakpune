Questions baddies ask

What is Peak Pune Baddies?

A name and a page for women in Pune who want plans, people and places with some attitude. Peak Pune is an unofficial guide to what is on in the city. It does not run a members club and it does not collect your details here.

Is there a Baddies group I can join?

Not yet. This page points you at plans and at the groups that already exist. If you want to start a women's circle, tell us.

I just moved to Pune. Where do I start?

Pick one plan near you and go twice. Familiar faces take a few visits. The new to Pune guide has a simple way to start.

Do I have to be social, fit or fun?

No. Come as you are. See the women's page for the no-pressure version.
