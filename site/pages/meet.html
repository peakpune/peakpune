How this works.

Three steps, no pressure.

  1. 1Say you are in. Name, WhatsApp number, your day and your pick of activity.
  2. 2We count. Saturday or Sunday, whichever gets more interest. The most wanted activity and the place follow.
  3. 3We announce. Day, place and plan go on the WhatsApp channel, then you get added to the meetup group.
No agenda. No pitch. No pressure.₹99 show-up fee, not for profit, and you get something back for it. Come alone or bring a friend. Say hi to people from the same city who got off their phones.
Who is organising this?

Shreya hosts, with Aakash as co-host. It is the first official Peak Pune meetup.

When and where is it?

This Saturday 28 or Sunday 29 Nov, whichever gets more interest. The place is TBC. We announce both on the WhatsApp channel.

Does it cost anything?

Joining the list is free. On the day there is a ₹99 show-up fee, paid by UPI. It is not for profit, and you get something back for it. Some activities may have their own ticket or entry cost, and we will say so before anyone commits.

What happens to my number?

It is only used to add you to the meetup WhatsApp group and message you once details are set. It is never shown on this site.
