See who in the Peak Pune community has a birthday coming up and what they would enjoy, then make it happen together. Celebrations here are member plans: made and hosted by members, not organised by Peak Pune.

How it works

Three steps between a birthday and a plan.

Step 1

Add your birthday

Real first and last name, WhatsApp number and Instagram handle are required. The owner uses them to check you are in the community and to reach you about your entry. They stay in the private review panel and are never published.

Step 2

Say what you would enjoy

Your day and month, your wish and your likes are what the wall shows. Your birth year is never shown, and your age appears on your card only if you tick the box.

Step 3

Members make the plan

Tap Plan something, Host it or Join the party on a card. Your name and number go to the owner privately, and he connects you on WhatsApp. Nothing is published, and no counts are shown.
