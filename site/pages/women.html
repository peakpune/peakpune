What Peak Pune does for you

We are a radar, not a gatekeeper. Here is what that means in practice.

01

You see who is behind it

Every plan links to the source it came from and shows whether the date or registration is confirmed. You know what you are walking into before you say yes.

02

A person reads what gets posted

Plans sent to Peak Pune are read by a person before they show. Public listings never carry a phone number or email.

03

You stay in charge

Groups run on their own channels, by their own admins. Read along first, ask questions, leave any time. Nobody needs your reason.

Safe community, on your terms

The city is yours at any hour. Sunrise run, midnight gig, late dinner. Peak Pune cannot vouch for any group, and no listing is a safety promise. What we can do is show you who is behind a plan and leave the call to you. A few habits a lot of women already swear by:

Know the plan

Check who is behind it

Every plan links to its source. Look at who is hosting before you say yes.

Backup

Tell a friend where you are going

Where, with whom, and when you will be back. Two minutes, done.

Exit

Know your way out

Have your ride sorted and leave when you want. You do not owe anyone a reason.

Gut

Trust the weird feeling

If something is off, walk away. No polite explanation needed.

Thrive your way

Do not shrink yourself to fit a plan. Pick what you would enjoy with nobody watching.

Move

Run, ride, climb

Early mornings, hill walks, a day on a trail. Show up at your own speed.

Make

Learn something with your hands

Workshops and creative evenings, where everyone is busy and talking is optional.

Go out

Gigs, films, a late dinner

Go with a plan and a ride home. Stay for one song or the whole night.

Running something for women?

A circle, a club, a weekly plan, a safe space. Send it in and we will take a look. Peak Pune does not list a women-only community yet, and we would like to.
