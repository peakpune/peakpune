How to make friends in Pune without forcing it

01

Go to something small

A morning run or a day trek beats a crowded party. Everyone is already doing the same thing, so there is always something to talk about.

02

Go back

Faces turn into names on the second visit. The third is when someone says "you again" and it feels good.

03

Say yes once

When someone asks if you want chai after, say yes. That is most of it.
