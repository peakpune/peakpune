Community

Watch together.

Four people, one show, one city. Pick a film, offer a few shows, and see who else across Pune can make it. The show that fills first is the one. Everyone then books their own tickets on District.

How it works

Three steps from "anyone up for this?" to the same row.

Step 1

Start a pool

Name the film, the day, how many you want, and up to three shows. Different cinemas are fine. That is how people from Baner and Kharadi find a common show.

Step 2

Join with your part of Pune

Tick the shows you can make and where you are coming from. You see the headcount for every show straight away. No name, number or sign-in.

Step 3

The first show to fill wins

When one show reaches the group size it says so, with a District link. Book adjacent seats, about two thirds back, in the centre block. Peak Pune holds no seats and takes no payment.

Open pools

Live as soon as they are started, and gone after the show time.

Start a pool

It goes live straight away. Keep it to a film and shows. Do not add names or numbers.

Peak Pune is a guide, not a booking service. It holds no seats, takes no payment and is not affiliated with District or any cinema. Check the show and price on District before you pay. To limit spam, the site stores a salted hash of your IP address, not the address itself. Anything that looks wrong can be reported on its card.
