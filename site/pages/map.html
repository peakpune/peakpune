Pune, on one map

The interactive map needs JavaScript and WebGL. Every place is listed below, and each place page links to its OpenStreetMap location.

Where the map comes from

Streets, rivers, parks, Metro stations and place shapes are OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), drawn by OpenMapTiles vector tiles served by OpenFreeMap, recoloured to the Peak Pune brand. Pins are Peak Pune listings whose coordinates come from the linked OpenStreetMap feature; the date each place was read is on its page. A pin is a map location, not a visit or a recommendation.
