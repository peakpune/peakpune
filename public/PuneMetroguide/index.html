The network today

Three lines, two operators. Only stations marked open are usable. Everything under construction or approved is listed further down and is not routable.

How to buy a ticket

Simplest first. Maha Metro and Line 3 sell tickets separately.

  1. WhatsApp e-ticket (Lines 1 and 2). The operator's homepage says to send "Hi" to book your e-ticket. Open WhatsAppneeds internet

    Line 3: its operator lists QR tickets through WhatsApp and its own app, but publishes no WhatsApp number on the pages we checked, so we do not list one. Some media reports say the same number offers a Line 3 option; that is unconfirmed.

  2. The Pune Metro app (Lines 1 and 2). Listed on the operator's homepage with store badges. Line 3 lists its own Line 3 app on its passenger amenities page.

  3. Station counters and ticket vending machines. Line 3's operator says tickets are sold by manual counter at every station and by ticket vending machine. Lines 1 and 2: counters and ticket vending machines at stations.

  4. One Pune Card (NCMC RuPay, tap in and tap out). Tap at the entry gate and again at the exit; the fare for the stations you travelled is deducted from the chip balance. KYC card: issue fee Rs 100, yearly Rs 75, minimum top-up Rs 50, chip balance up to Rs 2000, online top-up fee 1.8% of recharge value, capped at Rs 12. 20 contactless transactions per day (operator One Pune Card page). One Pune Card pageneeds internet

    Non-KYC card: Rs. 50 + taxes, yearly Rs. 75 + taxes, total balance up to Rs 3000. Non-KYC card pageneeds internet Line 3's operator lists NCMC cards, including the One Pune Card, among its ticket options.

What it costs

Lines 1 and 2 are one fare system: Rs 10 to Rs 35. Line 3 is a separate fare system: Rs 12 to Rs 62. There is no published combined fare for a trip across both, so the planner shows them separately and never adds them up.

Discounts the operator publishes

  • Get a 10% discount on all Metro ridesOperator homepage banner and the One Pune Card (KYC) page. The banner links to the One Pune Card page. The pages checked do not say which ticket types qualify. Operator pageneeds internet

The interchange: District Court

One station where Purple, Aqua and Line 3 meet.

  • Purple and Aqua: Purple and Aqua share the District Court station and one fare system; one ticket covers the change. The planner prices the whole trip from the Maha Metro fare table.
  • Line 3: Line 3 ends at District Court, Pune. The Line 3 operator says it has interchanges with Lines 1 and 2. It is a different operator with a separate fare system.

Not confirmed by either operator: media reports say a trip across the two systems currently needs two QR codes, that one NCMC card works on both, and that a single integrated ticket has been announced. How a single trip across both systems is charged is not published, so the planner shows each system's leg separately and no combined fare. Verify the current state with the operators before you travel.

Line 3's "Shivaji Nagar" station is not confirmed to be the same physical station as Line 1's "Shivaji Nagar", so the planner treats them as two stations and does not derive a change between them.
