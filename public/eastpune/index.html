East Pune

What this page means by East Pune

East Pune has no official boundary. This guide covers the band of neighbourhoods east of the city centre along Nagar Road and the Mula-Mutha river: Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Wadgaon Sheri, Kharadi, Mundhwa and Hadapsar with Magarpatta. The map covers a 3.4 km circle centred on Wadgaon Sheri: all of Kalyani Nagar, Wadgaon Sheri and Mundhwa fall inside it, while northern Viman Nagar, eastern Kharadi and parts of southern Hadapsar and Magarpatta do not, so those edges are under-covered. Koregaon Park sits at its western edge, so it is left out of this list. The airport corridor, the IT parks and the big malls are all in this band.

Getting to East Pune

Pune Metro's Aqua line runs along Nagar Road through the area. The operator's project profile, verified for the Pune Metro guide on 14 November 2026, lists two open stations in this area: Kalyani Nagar and Ramwadi, which is the eastern terminus. Viman Nagar and Kharadi have no Metro station today; the Ramwadi to Wagholi extension, approved by the Union Cabinet in June 2025, would serve them, but no opening date is confirmed, so Ramwadi plus a short road hop is the current route. Fares, line changes and ticketing: the Pune Metro guide.

Pune airport sits just north of Viman Nagar, which makes this the closest part of the city to the terminal. Pune Junction railway station is west of Koregaon Park; Hadapsar has a suburban station on the line towards Daund. The map circle stops short of Aditi Gardens in the middle of Magarpatta, so that park is described in the Hadapsar section but not pinned. Distances depend on where in East Pune you are headed, so route them from the map.

Kalyani Nagar

Wikipedia describes Kalyani Nagar as an affluent, predominantly residential neighbourhood in eastern Pune, administered from the Nagar Road-Wadgaonsheri ward office. For visitors it is two things: the Aga Khan Palace on Nagar Road, and the restaurant and bar belt covered below.

South of the neighbourhood proper, along the Mula-Mutha around the Westin, High Spirits and The KOPA mall, sits a riverfront pocket of restaurants and lounges. Listings often file it under Kalyani Nagar; on this page it shows as Kalyani Nagar riverfront. Kalyani Nagar Metro station on the Aqua line serves the area.

Sources: Wikipedia, Kalyani Nagar, OpenStreetMap. Read 24 November 2026.

Viman Nagar

A residential and retail neighbourhood close to the airport, described by Wikipedia as part of the eastern corridor with Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar. Phoenix Marketcity is its anchor, and Udaan Biodiversity Park its green space.

Two Metro stations, Viman Nagar and Somnath Nagar, sit on its side of Nagar Road.

Sources: Wikipedia, Viman Nagar. Read 24 November 2026.

Wadgaon Sheri

A residential neighbourhood off Nagar Road between Kalyani Nagar and Viman Nagar. Wikipedia notes it was a village outside the city and is now an upmarket residential area bounded by Nagar Road to the north and the Mula-Mutha to the south.

Ramwadi, the eastern end of the operational Metro line, is on its edge.

Sources: Wikipedia, Wadgaon Sheri. Read 24 November 2026.

Kharadi

Wikipedia describes Kharadi as a suburb on the banks of the Mula-Mutha that serves as a hub for information technology and business parks. The office parks brought the restaurants: the main roads through Kharadi have one of the densest dining stretches in this guide.

Kharadi Bypass Metro station is mapped on the Aqua line extension beyond Ramwadi; check the Pune Metro guide for what is operational before planning around it.

Sources: Wikipedia, Kharadi. Read 24 November 2026.

Mundhwa

South of Kalyani Nagar and Wadgaon Sheri, along the river road towards Kharadi. The KOPA mall and the riverside restaurants around it are the landmarks here, and the area absorbs the overflow of the Koregaon Park night-out crowd.

OpenStreetMap coverage is thinner here than in Kharadi, so the list for Mundhwa is shorter than the reality.

Sources: OpenStreetMap. Read 24 November 2026.

Hadapsar and Magarpatta

Wikipedia describes Hadapsar as a developed eastern suburb that grew into a major industrial area from the 1990s. Its best-known piece today is Magarpatta City, the 182-hectare township with Aditi Gardens and Seasons Mall, with Amanora's township and mall next door.

Hadapsar also has a suburban railway station on the Pune to Daund line.

Sources: Wikipedia, Hadapsar, Wikipedia, Magarpatta. Read 24 November 2026.

Worth knowing

Described from each place's own site or a named publication. Peak Pune has not visited or ranked them.

Landmark · Nagar Road, Kalyani Nagar

Aga Khan Palace

Built by Sultan Muhammed Shah Aga Khan III as a famine-relief work project, over five years, according to its Wikipedia article. During the Quit India movement it served as a prison for Mahatma Gandhi, Kasturba Gandhi, Mahadev Desai and Sarojini Naidu. Kasturba Gandhi and Mahadev Desai died here, and their samadhis are on the grounds.

The Archaeological Survey of India declared it a monument of national importance in 2003. Check current opening times and entry fee with the monument before visiting.

Sources: Wikipedia, Aga Khan Palace, Gandhi National Memorial Society. Read 24 November 2026.

Park · Viman Nagar

Udaan Biodiversity Park

A nature park in Viman Nagar turned from barren rocky land into a green habitat, with a lake, a waterfall and plant name boards in English, Marathi and Braille, according to PuneDiary. OpenStreetMap maps it off the Viman Nagar road grid.

Check entry rules locally before going.

Sources: PuneDiary, Udaan Biodiversity Park, OpenStreetMap. Read 24 November 2026.

Eating out · Nagar Road to the riverfront, Kalyani Nagar

The Kalyani Nagar restaurant belt

Bars and cafes cluster around Nagar Road and the lanes behind it, with hotel dining along the river. Names on the map include Cobbler & Crew, Lord of the Drinks, The Flour Works, Cafe Pondi, Yeti and Farzi Cafe.

The riverfront pocket south of the neighbourhood proper, around the Westin and The KOPA, holds more restaurants and lounges, including High Spirits for live music. Addresses and hours come from OpenStreetMap; check the venue before you go.

Sources: OpenStreetMap, High Spirits. Read 24 November 2026.

Mall · Viman Nagar

Phoenix Marketcity

The big mall on Nagar Road in Viman Nagar, with national and international brands, a food court and multiplex. OpenStreetMap maps the complex as Phoenix Avenue of Stars; the mall's own site lists stores, dining and cinema timings.

Viman Nagar Metro station (Aqua line) is a short walk away, which makes it the easiest mall in this guide to reach without a car.

Sources: Phoenix Marketcity Pune, OpenStreetMap. Read 24 November 2026.

Township and malls · Hadapsar

Magarpatta City, Seasons Mall and Amanora

Magarpatta City is a 182-hectare gated township in Hadapsar with homes, offices, a hospital, schools, the 10-hectare Aditi Gardens park and Seasons Mall, according to Wikipedia. The developer's own site tells the township story. About 30% of the area is green cover.

Next door, Amanora is another large township and mall complex. Together they make Hadapsar the southern shopping anchor of East Pune, with restaurants in and around both malls.

Sources: Wikipedia, Magarpatta, Magarpatta City, Amanora Mall. Read 24 November 2026.

Eating out · Kharadi South Main Road and Fountain Road

Kharadi's main-road dining

Kharadi is Pune's IT-and-business-park hub on the Mula-Mutha, and its dining follows the office crowd: restaurants, bars and cafes line the main roads, from thali places to brewpubs and burger joints. The names on the map skew local and unpretentious.

New places open often here, so treat any list, including this one, as a snapshot. OpenStreetMap contributors keep the map current; each name links to its entry.

Sources: Wikipedia, Kharadi, OpenStreetMap. Read 24 November 2026.

Where this list comes from

Names, cuisines, street names and opening hours come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), read on 24 November 2026. Places within a 3.4 km circle centred on Wadgaon Sheri, chosen to cover the neighbourhoods this page treats as East Pune: Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Kharadi, Wadgaon Sheri, Mundhwa and Hadapsar (including Magarpatta). Neighbourhood tags are assigned from OpenStreetMap polygons where they exist and hand-drawn boxes on OpenStreetMap roads elsewhere. "East Pune" has no official boundary, so the tag is approximate. Koregaon Park is excluded on purpose. OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers. Peak Pune did not visit or rank these places, so a venue may have closed, moved or changed its hours. Check with the venue before you go. Each name links to its OpenStreetMap entry, where anyone can fix it. The map itself is drawn from OpenStreetMap data by OpenMapTiles vector tiles served by OpenFreeMap. To report a mistake on this page, use the contact on the about page.
