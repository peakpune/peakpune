Dry lines about real timings and real trails, each tied to a place we have checked. Reader submissions are not open yet.

8:30 AM, when it opens

The shutters go up at 8:30. MG Road has opinions about who was in the queue first.

Marz-O-Rin · All year

10:00 AM to 8:30 PM

Marathi books until 8:30 PM. Long enough to walk in for one and leave with a bag.

Akshardhara Book Gallery · All year

Before the 5:30 PM closing

Open all year, except the three days everyone in Pune already has other plans.

Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum · All year; closed 26 January, 15 August and Anant Chaturdashi

On the half hour

The trains run every half hour. Provided four of you turn up. Pune has always believed in quorum.

Joshi's Museum of Miniature Railways · All year

5:00 AM, at opening

Doors open at 5 AM. On Tuesdays they stay open half an hour longer. Even the schedule has a favourite day.

Shreemant Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple · All year

4:30 AM

Mangala aarti at 4:30 AM. The rest of Kondhwa is still negotiating with its alarm.

ISKCON NVCC Pune · All year

11 AM to 8 PM

Closed Mondays. Also the first and third Sundays. Carry a calendar and a backup plan.

Gyaan Adab · All year

Early morning, daylight only

9.47 km of dirt path and no street lights. The tekdi keeps its own timings too.

Vetal Tekdi and ARAI hill loop · All year; unlit paths

Early morning

One loop, two neighbourhoods, one bridge crossed twice. 8.62 km to settle which side has better coffee.

Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar loop · All year

Early start; Saturdays are quieter in monsoon

Half of Pune did its first fort here. The other half is on the steps right now.

Lohagad Fort · July to February

Start by 7 AM

The guide says do not make this your first trek. Pune will, of course, consider it a suggestion.

Torna Fort · September to February

Early in summer; little shade

A fort with two names and very few visitors. Both facts suit it.
