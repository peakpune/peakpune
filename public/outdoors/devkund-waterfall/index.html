Devkund Waterfall

Mostly flat forest walk from Bhira to a plunge pool under a tall waterfall near Tamhini ghat. Swimming is not allowed, per the guide.

Easy

From Pune
About 100 km, 2.5–3 hours by road
Time on trail
1.5–2 hours each way
Starts at
Bhira, near Tamhini ghat
Best months
September to January
Good to know
Guide gives the walk as about 9-10 km there and back; left out of distance_km because it is a range.
