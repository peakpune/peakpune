Harishchandragad

Long Sahyadri trek to Konkan Kada and the Kedareshwar cave, approached from Pachnai or Khireshwar.

Challenging

From Pune
About 165 km, 4 hours by road
Time on trail
2–3 hours up from Pachnai
Starts at
Pachnai (Akole side, shortest) or Khireshwar (Malshej side)
Best months
August to February
Good to know
Long drive for a single day; the source lists it under treks near Pune.

Where this comes from

Distances, timings and difficulty are as published by Pune Trekkers, read on 2026-11-13. Conditions change, and nobody at Peak Pune has walked or run this for you. Go early, go in company, and check with the source first.
