Day trip from Pune · Khadakwasla

Khadakwasla Dam

Dam on the Mutha river south-west of the city, listed by the district as a scenic spot reachable by city bus.

From Pune
About 15 km
Good to know
District page says about 15 km from Pune city (its summary says 20 km), with buses from Swargate, Pune station and Shivajinagar. Travel time not stated, so the DAY_TRIP label is incomplete.

Open the district page

Where this comes from

Distances, timings and difficulty are as published by Pune district administration, read on 2026-11-13. Conditions change, and nobody at Peak Pune has walked or run this for you. Go early, go in company, and check with the source first.
