Ghoradeshwar

Short morning hill and cave hike off the old Mumbai-Pune highway near Talegaon; exposed, so the guide suggests an early start.

Easy

From Pune
About 35 km, under an hour by road
Time on trail
About 45 minutes to 1 hour up
Starts at
Ghorawadi, near Somatane on the old Mumbai–Pune highway
