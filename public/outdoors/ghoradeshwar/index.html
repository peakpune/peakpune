Ghoradeshwar

Short morning hill and cave hike off the old Mumbai-Pune highway near Talegaon; exposed, so the guide suggests an early start.

Easy

From Pune
About 35 km, under an hour by road
Time on trail
About 45 minutes to 1 hour up
Starts at
Ghorawadi, near Somatane on the old Mumbai–Pune highway

Read the full guide on Pune Trekkers

Where this comes from

Distances, timings and difficulty are as published by Pune Trekkers, read on 2026-11-13. Conditions change, and nobody at Peak Pune has walked or run this for you. Go early, go in company, and check with the source first.
