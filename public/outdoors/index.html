Running loops in Pune

Plotted by PuneRunners Club on OpenStreetMap data. Mapped, not run-tested: footpaths, traffic and lighting are unchecked.

Magarpatta

Magarpatta circle road lap

Short lap of the Magarpatta circle road; the page builds eight longer lines, up to 50 km, from the same start point.

1.31 km loop

Kharadi

Kharadi loop

Road loop on Kharadi South Main Road, Fountain Road and Downtown Road near the EON IT park.

5.73 km loop

Pashan / Baner

Pashan and Baner hill loop

Trail loop linking the Pashan and Baner hill trails with short stretches of Pancard Club Road.

6.11 km loop

Viman Nagar

Viman Nagar loop

Road loop round the Viman Nagar blocks using Airport New Road, Symbiosis Law School Road and Viman Nagar Road.

6.17 km loop

Magarpatta

Magarpatta City loop

Loop on West Gate Road, township roads and footpaths inside Magarpatta City, a private township.

6.2 km loop

Savitribai Phule Pune University

SPPU campus loop

Loop on campus roads inside Savitribai Phule Pune University, from the main gate towards IUCAA and the hostel roads.

7.03 km loop

Koregaon Park

Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar loop

Road loop along Burning Ghat Road and North Main Road, over Aga Khan Bridge to the Kalyani Nagar avenues, then back via Lane 7 and South Main Road.

8.62 km loop

Vetal Tekdi

Vetal Tekdi and ARAI hill loop

Trail loop on dirt paths over Vetal Tekdi and south to the ARAI hill; almost entirely paths and tracks.

9.47 km loop

Treks and day trips near Pune

Nearest first. Distances and drive times are from Pune Trekkers' volunteer-written guides.

Haveli

Katraj to Sinhagad (K2S)

Ridge traverse of about 13 km over a dozen hills from Katraj ghat to Sinhagad, often done at night.

Challenging12 km from Pune

Khadakwasla

Khadakwasla Dam

Dam on the Mutha river south-west of the city, listed by the district as a scenic spot reachable by city bus.

20 km from Pune

Maval

Ghoradeshwar

Short morning hill and cave hike off the old Mumbai-Pune highway near Talegaon; exposed, so the guide suggests an early start.

Easy35 km from Pune · under an hour by road

Haveli

Sinhagad Fort

Steady, rocky climb of about 2.7 km from Atkarwadi; a ghat road also reaches most of the way up.

Moderate35 km from Pune · about an hour by road

Velhe

Rajgad Fort

Long climb to Padmavati Machi on Shivaji Maharaj's first capital, with three large spurs to explore on top.

Moderate60 km from Pune · 1.5–2 hours by road

Bhor

Rohida Fort

Short, quiet fort above Bhor with carved gateways and cisterns; also called Vichitragad.

Easy60 km from Pune · 1.5 hours by road

Lonavala

Lohagad Fort

Short climb on stone steps from Lohagadwadi through four gateways, or a longer walk from Malavli station; a common first fort.

Easy65 km from Pune · 1.5–2 hours by road

Velhe

Torna Fort

Long, steep climb from Velhe along rocky ridges; the guide advises against it as a first trek.

Challenging65 km from Pune · 2 hours by road

Lonavala

Visapur Fort

Larger, quieter neighbour of Lohagad with a big plateau; in monsoon, streams run down the old steps.

Easy65 km from Pune · 1.5–2 hours by road

Pawna

Tung Fort

Short, steep cone above Pawna lake with an exposed final climb.

Moderate90 km from Pune · 2–2.5 hours by road

Bhira

Devkund Waterfall

Mostly flat forest walk from Bhira to a plunge pool under a tall waterfall near Tamhini ghat. Swimming is not allowed, per the guide.

Easy100 km from Pune · 2.5–3 hours by road

Panvel

Kalavantin Durg & Prabalgad

Pinnacle near Panvel reached by steep rock-cut steps without railings, beside the forested Prabalgad plateau.

Challenging120 km from Pune · 2.5–3 hours by road

Pali

Sudhagad Fort

Forested fort near Pali with ladders, a wide plateau and an old wada on top.

Moderate120 km from Pune · 3 hours by road

Junnar

Jivdhan Fort

Fort above the old Naneghat pass with rock-cut steps, rock patches and the Vanarlingi pinnacle.

Moderate125 km from Pune · 3–3.5 hours by road

Khireshwar

Aadrai Jungle

Monsoon forest walk near Khireshwar with streams and waterfalls; the group does it with a local guide.

Moderate130 km from Pune · 3.5–4 hours by road

Badlapur

Chanderi Fort

Climb to the Chanderi caves, with a harder scree and rock scramble above them to the summit.

Challenging130 km from Pune · 3 hours by road

Khireshwar

Kalu Waterfall

Forest trek near Khireshwar to a tall waterfall below Harishchandragad.

Moderate160 km from Pune · 4 hours by road

Malshej

Harishchandragad

Long Sahyadri trek to Konkan Kada and the Kedareshwar cave, approached from Pachnai or Khireshwar.

Challenging165 km from Pune · 4 hours by road

Bari

Kalsubai Peak

Long steady climb from Bari with iron ladders near the top; the guide gives the summit as 1,646 m, Maharashtra's highest point.

Moderate180 km from Pune · 4–5 hours by road

Bhandardara

Ratangad Fort

Forest climb from Ratanwadi with ladders, caves on top and a natural rock window called Nedhe.

Moderate190 km from Pune · 4.5–5 hours by road
