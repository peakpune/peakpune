Aadrai Jungle

Monsoon forest walk near Khireshwar with streams and waterfalls; the group does it with a local guide.

Moderate

From Pune
About 130 km, 3.5–4 hours by road
Time on trail
4–6 hours return
Starts at
Khireshwar, near Pimpalgaon Joga dam
Best months
July to September
Good to know
Long drive for a single day; the source lists it under treks near Pune.
