Day trip from Pune · Lonavala

Lohagad Fort

Short climb on stone steps from Lohagadwadi through four gateways, or a longer walk from Malavli station; a common first fort.

Easy

From Pune
About 65 km, 1.5–2 hours by road
Time on trail
30–45 minutes up from Lohagadwadi
Starts at
Lohagadwadi (by road) or Malavli (by train)
Best months
July to February

Read the full guide on Pune Trekkers

Where this comes from

Distances, timings and difficulty are as published by Pune Trekkers, read on 2026-11-13. Conditions change, and nobody at Peak Pune has walked or run this for you. Go early, go in company, and check with the source first.
