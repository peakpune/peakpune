Where the map comes from

Streets, rivers, parks, Metro stations and place shapes are OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), drawn by OpenMapTiles vector tiles served by OpenFreeMap, recoloured to the Peak Pune brand. Pins are Peak Pune listings whose coordinates come from the linked OpenStreetMap feature; the date we checked each place is on its page. A pin is a map location, not a visit or a recommendation.
