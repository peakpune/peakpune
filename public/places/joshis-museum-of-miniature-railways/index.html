Joshi's Museum of Miniature Railways

Working model city with miniature trains, run as a 25-minute narrated show every half hour. A minimum group size is needed for a show to run.

  • families
  • kids
  • rainy day
Address
17/1 B/2 Corner Of Bhau Joshi And G A Kulkarni Road, Near Karishma Society, Kothrud, Pune-411038
Hours
Weekdays 9am-5pm; Thursday 9am-1pm; Saturday 9am-4pm and 5pm-8pm; Sunday 5pm-8pm; lunch break 1:00-1:30 pm; shows every half hour
Prices
Rs. 120 per head above 3 years; free below 3 years. The Pune museum page requires at least 4 visitors for a show. Confirm current arrangements with the museum.
Area
Kothrud, Pune
Map
18.501412, 73.822364 © OpenStreetMap contributors
Good to know
Site is inconsistent on minimum group size: Pune page says 4, home page says 3. OpenStreetMap opening_hours agree with the site's timetable.
