Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum

Museum on Bajirao Road with a ticketed collection; its visit page lists daily hours, entry fees by age and nationality, and annual closure days.

  • history

Address
No. 1377/78, Kamal Kunj, Bajirao Rd, Natu Baag, Shukrawar Peth, Pune, Maharashtra 411002
Hours
10:00 am to 5:30 pm daily; closed 26 January, 15 August, Anant Chaturdashi and the next day; festival timings differ (call to confirm)
Prices
Rs. 120 adult (above 12); Rs. 50 child (below 12); Rs. 400 foreign adult; Rs. 100 foreign child; free for blind and differently abled visitors
