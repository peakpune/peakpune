Heritage and history in Pune

Shivajinagar

Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute

Research institute for Indology with courses and a heritage festival listed on its site. Visitor access was not confirmed.

Nagar Road

Aga Khan Palace

Palace with arches and lawns where Mahatma Gandhi, Kasturba Gandhi and Mahadev Desai were held during the Quit India Movement; it has marble memorials.

Kasba Peth

Lal Mahal

Reconstruction of the 1630 palace where Shivaji Maharaj spent his early years, in the centre of the old city.

Kasba Peth

Shaniwar Wada

Fortified seat of the Peshwas, built in 1736 and gutted by fire in 1828; walls and gates remain. The linked district tourism page describes the site.

Temples in Pune

Budhwar Peth

Shreemant Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple

Ganpati temple run by a public trust in Budhwar Peth; the official site publishes daily darshan timings and an aarti schedule.

5:00 AM - 10:30 PM; Tuesdays 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM

Kasba Peth

Shri Kasba Ganpati

Kasba Peth Ganpati temple, described on its own site as the first of Pune's honoured (manacha) Ganpatis.

Kondhwa Budruk

ISKCON NVCC Pune

ISKCON temple campus on Katraj-Kondhwa Road with a published daily aarti and darshan schedule, a guest house and a Govinda's restaurant site.

Mangala Aarti from 04:30 AM; darshan closed 1:10 PM - 4:30 PM;…

Bookstores in Pune

Baner

Pagdandi Bookstore & Coffee

Independent bookstore with a coffee counter on Baner Pashan Link Road. The site also sells books online and lists a daily support line.

Shukrawar Peth

Akshardhara Book Gallery

Marathi-focused bookshop on Bajirao Road that also runs an online store and book exhibitions.

Store time 10:00 AM to 8:30 PM (days not stated)

Galleries in Pune

Koregaon Park

Monalisa Kalagram

Art gallery and culture space at the end of South Main Road, showing rotating exhibitions.

Cultural centres in Pune

Kalyani Nagar

Gyaan Adab

Literary and cultural centre hosting book clubs, Urdu and Hindi sessions and arts events.

11am to 8pm; closed Mondays and 1st and 3rd Sundays

Archives in Pune

Law College Road

NFDC - National Film Archive of India

India's national film archive on Law College Road, now under NFDC. Public screening details were not confirmed in this pass.

Cafes in Pune

MG Road (Pune Cantonment)

Marz-O-Rin

Long-running MG Road fast-food cafe in a heritage building, established 1965 per its site, with sandwiches, cakes, pastries and milkshakes.

Mon-Sun 8:30 AM - 10:00 PM

NIBM Road

Cafe Peter

Cafe on NIBM Road serving a mix of Indian, Korean and British dishes, per its site.

Restaurants in Pune

Koregaon Park

Malaka Spice (Koregaon Park)

South-East Asian restaurant in Koregaon Park's Lane 5; the brand describes a farm-to-table approach through its own Cherish Farm.

Kharadi

Malaka Spice (Kharadi)

Kharadi outlet of the South-East Asian restaurant brand, on the first floor of Global High Street in EON Free Zone.

Baner

Malaka Spice (Baner)

Baner outlet of the South-East Asian restaurant brand, on Pan Card Club Road.

Koregaon Park

Arthur's Theme (Koregaon Park)

European-style restaurant that opened in Koregaon Park in 1997, per its site; the menu with prices is published online.

Mon-Sun 11:30 am - 11:30 pm (listed once for the brand)

Baner

Arthur's Theme (Baner)

Balewadi High Street outlet of the Koregaon Park restaurant brand.

Mon-Sun 11:30 am - 11:30 pm (listed once for the brand)

FC Road

Wadeshwar (FC Road)

Deccan Gymkhana

Hotel Shreyas

Apte Road hotel, operating since 1966 per its site, with a vegetarian Maharashtrian restaurant serving thali.

Bars and brewpubs in Pune

Koregaon Park Annexe

Doolally Taproom (Koregaon Park)

Taproom of a craft beer company that describes its spaces as having beer, pets and board games; also hosts ticketed events.

Kalyani Nagar

Toit Pune

Brewpub in Kalyani Nagar, open since 2019 on a former horse-stable site per its page; serves breakfast on Sundays.

Sweets and snacks in Pune

Deccan Gymkhana

Chitale Bandhu Mithaiwale (Deccan)

Deccan branch of the Pune sweets and snacks maker; the brand's store locator lists it among many city outlets.

Parks and gardens in Pune

Swargate

Sarasbaug and Peshwe Park

Lawned gardens near Swargate with a jogging track and a Ganesh temple on a hillock in a lake; adjoining Peshwe Park has boat rides and a mini train.

Zoo in Pune

Katraj

Rajiv Gandhi Zoological Park and Wildlife Research Centre

Municipal zoo at Katraj with around 60 species grouped by ecosystem, plus battery-car transport and wheelchairs for visitors, per the district page.
