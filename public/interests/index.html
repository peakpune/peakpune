Treks and forts

For people who want a summit, a fort or a night out on a hill.

Trek · Khireshwar

Aadrai Jungle

Monsoon forest walk near Khireshwar with streams and waterfalls; the group does it with a local guide.

Trek · Badlapur

Chanderi Fort

Climb to the Chanderi caves, with a harder scree and rock scramble above them to the summit.

Trek · Bhira

Devkund Waterfall

Mostly flat forest walk from Bhira to a plunge pool under a tall waterfall near Tamhini ghat. Swimming is not allowed, per the guide.

Trek · Maval

Ghoradeshwar

Short morning hill and cave hike off the old Mumbai-Pune highway near Talegaon; exposed, so the guide suggests an early start.

Trek · Malshej

Harishchandragad

Long Sahyadri trek to Konkan Kada and the Kedareshwar cave, approached from Pachnai or Khireshwar.

Trek · Junnar

Jivdhan Fort

Fort above the old Naneghat pass with rock-cut steps, rock patches and the Vanarlingi pinnacle.

Trek · Panvel

Kalavantin Durg & Prabalgad

Pinnacle near Panvel reached by steep rock-cut steps without railings, beside the forested Prabalgad plateau.

Trek · Bari

Kalsubai Peak

Long steady climb from Bari with iron ladders near the top; the guide gives the summit as 1,646 m, Maharashtra's highest point.

Trek · Khireshwar

Kalu Waterfall

Forest trek near Khireshwar to a tall waterfall below Harishchandragad.

Running

City loops, trail runs and races.

Run · Koregaon Park

Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar loop

Road loop along Burning Ghat Road and North Main Road, over Aga Khan Bridge to the Kalyani Nagar avenues, then back via Lane 7 and South Main Road.

Run · Viman Nagar

Viman Nagar loop

Road loop round the Viman Nagar blocks using Airport New Road, Symbiosis Law School Road and Viman Nagar Road.

Run · Magarpatta

Magarpatta City loop

Loop on West Gate Road, township roads and footpaths inside Magarpatta City, a private township.

Run · Kharadi

Kharadi loop

Road loop on Kharadi South Main Road, Fountain Road and Downtown Road near the EON IT park.

Run · Savitribai Phule Pune University

SPPU campus loop

Loop on campus roads inside Savitribai Phule Pune University, from the main gate towards IUCAA and the hostel roads.

Run · Vetal Tekdi

Vetal Tekdi and ARAI hill loop

Trail loop on dirt paths over Vetal Tekdi and south to the ARAI hill; almost entirely paths and tracks.

Run · Pashan / Baner

Pashan and Baner hill loop

Trail loop linking the Pashan and Baner hill trails with short stretches of Pancard Club Road.

Run · Magarpatta

Magarpatta circle road lap

Short lap of the Magarpatta circle road; the page builds eight longer lines, up to 50 km, from the same start point.

PuneRunners Club

Books and reading

Bookstores, libraries, archives and book clubs.

Place · Baner

Pagdandi Bookstore & Coffee

Independent bookstore with a coffee counter on Baner Pashan Link Road. The site also sells books online and lists a daily support line.

Place · Shukrawar Peth

Akshardhara Book Gallery

Marathi-focused bookshop on Bajirao Road that also runs an online store and book exhibitions.

Place · Kalyani Nagar

Gyaan Adab

Literary and cultural centre hosting book clubs, Urdu and Hindi sessions and arts events.

Place · Law College Road

NFDC - National Film Archive of India

India's national film archive on Law College Road, now under NFDC. Public screening details were not confirmed in this pass.

Place · Shivajinagar

Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute

Research institute for Indology with courses and a heritage festival listed on its site. Visitor access was not confirmed.

Community · Baner

We Barroom The Lit Club

Book club run with Pagdandi; edition 18 discusses To Kill a Mockingbird at We Idliwale Barroom near Balewadi High Street.

Community · Koregaon Park

The Malaka Book Club

Book club session with a meal inspired by the book, hosted by Malaka Spice with Pagdandi; registration is through a ticketing listing.

Community · Baner

Pagdandi Book Club

The bookstore's own book club, meeting at the shop on a Saturday morning.

History and heritage

Museums, old wadas and places with a past.

Place · Shukrawar Peth

Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum

Museum on Bajirao Road with a ticketed collection; its visit page lists daily hours, entry fees by age and nationality, and annual closure days.

Place · Kothrud

Joshi's Museum of Miniature Railways

Working model city with miniature trains, run as a narrated show every half hour.

Place · Budhwar Peth

Shreemant Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple

Ganpati temple run by a public trust in Budhwar Peth; the official site publishes daily darshan timings and an aarti schedule.

Place · Kasba Peth

Shri Kasba Ganpati

Kasba Peth Ganpati temple, described on its own site as the first of Pune's honoured (manacha) Ganpatis.

Place · Law College Road

NFDC - National Film Archive of India

India's national film archive on Law College Road, now under NFDC. Public screening details were not confirmed in this pass.

Place · Shivajinagar

Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute

Research institute for Indology with courses and a heritage festival listed on its site. Visitor access was not confirmed.

Place · Nagar Road

Aga Khan Palace

Palace with arches and lawns where Mahatma Gandhi, Kasturba Gandhi and Mahadev Desai were held during the Quit India Movement; it has marble memorials.

Place · Kasba Peth

Lal Mahal

Reconstruction of the 1630 palace where Shivaji Maharaj spent his early years, in the centre of the old city.

Place · Kasba Peth

Shaniwar Wada

Fortified seat of the Peshwas, built in 1736 and gutted by fire in 1828; walls and gates remain. The linked district tourism page describes the site.

Food and drink

Restaurants, cafes, sweets and brewpubs.

Place · Baner

Pagdandi Bookstore & Coffee

Independent bookstore with a coffee counter on Baner Pashan Link Road. The site also sells books online and lists a daily support line.

Place · MG Road (Pune Cantonment)

Marz-O-Rin

Long-running MG Road fast-food cafe in a heritage building, established 1965 per its site, with sandwiches, cakes, pastries and milkshakes.

Place · Koregaon Park

Malaka Spice (Koregaon Park)

South-East Asian restaurant in Koregaon Park's Lane 5; the brand describes a farm-to-table approach through its own Cherish Farm.

Place · Kharadi

Malaka Spice (Kharadi)

Kharadi outlet of the South-East Asian restaurant brand, on the first floor of Global High Street in EON Free Zone.

Place · Baner

Malaka Spice (Baner)

Baner outlet of the South-East Asian restaurant brand, on Pan Card Club Road.

Place · Koregaon Park

Arthur's Theme (Koregaon Park)

European-style restaurant that opened in Koregaon Park in 1997, per its site; the menu with prices is published online.

Place · Baner

Arthur's Theme (Baner)

Balewadi High Street outlet of the Koregaon Park restaurant brand.

Place · Koregaon Park Annexe

Doolally Taproom (Koregaon Park)

Taproom of a craft beer company that describes its spaces as having beer, pets and board games; also hosts ticketed events.

Place · Kalyani Nagar

Toit Pune

Brewpub in Kalyani Nagar, open since 2019 on a former horse-stable site per its page; serves breakfast on Sundays.

Art, film and making things

Galleries, cultural centres and workshops.

Place · Kalyani Nagar

Gyaan Adab

Literary and cultural centre hosting book clubs, Urdu and Hindi sessions and arts events.

Place · Law College Road

NFDC - National Film Archive of India

India's national film archive on Law College Road, now under NFDC. Public screening details were not confirmed in this pass.

Place · Koregaon Park

Monalisa Kalagram

Art gallery and culture space at the end of South Main Road, showing rotating exhibitions.

Board games and tech

Communities that meet to play or build.

Place · Koregaon Park Annexe

Doolally Taproom (Koregaon Park)

Taproom of a craft beer company that describes its spaces as having beer, pets and board games; also hosts ticketed events.

Community

Google Developer Group Pune

Volunteer-run developer community holding talks, workshops, study jams and an annual DevFest; the chapter page shows about 6,300 members.

Parks and green Pune

Gardens, lakes and open space inside the city.

Place · Nagar Road

Aga Khan Palace

Palace with arches and lawns where Mahatma Gandhi, Kasturba Gandhi and Mahadev Desai were held during the Quit India Movement; it has marble memorials.

Place · Swargate

Sarasbaug and Peshwe Park

Lawned gardens near Swargate with a jogging track and a Ganesh temple on a hillock in a lake; adjoining Peshwe Park has boat rides and a mini train.

Place · Katraj

Rajiv Gandhi Zoological Park and Wildlife Research Centre

Municipal zoo at Katraj with around 60 species grouped by ecosystem, plus battery-car transport and wheelchairs for visitors, per the district page.

Trek · Khadakwasla

Khadakwasla Dam

Dam on the Mutha river south-west of the city, listed by the district as a scenic spot reachable by city bus.
