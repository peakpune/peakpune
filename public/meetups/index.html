7 Pune groups with a public joining page and dated activity, read on 2026-11-13. Rules, costs and group links live on their own sites.

running · walking

PuneRunners Club

Free running community for runners, joggers and walkers of every pace; runs and race plans are arranged in its WhatsApp community.

No fixed public meet point. Runs are planned in the WhatsApp community; the Strava club page mentions weekly runs and long runs.

trekking · hiking

Pune Trekkers

Free, volunteer-run trekking group of about 2,300 members across three WhatsApp groups. Trek costs are shared at actuals with no markup.

Weekend treks posted on the trek board; register per trek and wait for the coordinator to confirm.

ActiveMembership free; you pay your share of actual trek expenses

How to join

travel · trekking

Karawaan community

WhatsApp community of a Pune travel company whose paid treks, camps and trips are planned for people joining alone.

Trips are announced in the WhatsApp community and listed on the trips page.

ActiveCommunity is free to join; trips are paid, priced per trip

How to join
