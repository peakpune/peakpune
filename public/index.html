The manifesto

Pune is not boring. You are just looking in the wrong places.

The best nights in this city never make the listings sites. They move through group chats and word of mouth. A gig in a basement. A food popup that exists for one Sunday. A trek that leaves while the city sleeps.

PeakPune is the radar. It exists to find the few things in Pune worth leaving the house for. It is not a directory. It will never list everything.

Plans on PeakPune have one home. Members can host their own plans; a member plan appears only after the host and the plan are approved. Anything hosted by PeakPune itself will be marked as an official PeakPune plan.

Places, with sources

Each place links to its source. Hours and prices are shown only where the source states them; confirm details before visiting.

All 28 places
Museum · Shukrawar Peth

Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum

Museum on Bajirao Road with a ticketed collection; its visit page lists daily hours, entry fees by age and nationality, and annual closure days.

Bookstore · Baner

Pagdandi Bookstore & Coffee

Independent bookstore with a coffee counter on Baner Pashan Link Road. The site also sells books online and lists a daily support line.

Cafe · MG Road (Pune Cantonment)

Marz-O-Rin

Long-running MG Road fast-food cafe in a heritage building, established 1965 per its site, with sandwiches, cakes, pastries and milkshakes.

Museum · Kothrud

Joshi's Museum of Miniature Railways

Working model city with miniature trains, run as a narrated show every half hour.

Heritage site · Kasba Peth

Shaniwar Wada

Fortified seat of the Peshwas, built in 1736 and gutted by fire in 1828; walls and gates remain. The linked district tourism page describes the site.

Bar and brewpub · Kalyani Nagar

Toit Pune

Brewpub in Kalyani Nagar, open since 2019 on a former horse-stable site per its page; serves breakfast on Sundays.

Start early. The city is cooler.

Quick answers about Pune

What is Peak Pune?

Peak Pune is Pune's unofficial guide to doing interesting shit: a city radar with a short list of things worth leaving the house for, plus places, running loops, treks and meetups. We do not organise runs or treks; we point to the people who do. About Peak Pune

Which places in Pune can I visit?

Peak Pune lists 28 places in Pune, including museums, bookstores, cafes, restaurants, bars and brewpubs. Each page gives the address, timings and entry fee where the venue states them, with a link to the venue's own page and the date it was read. All 28 places

Where can I run in Pune?

Start with the 8 mapped city loops, from 1.3 to 9.5 km, plotted by PuneRunners Club: Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar loop, Viman Nagar loop, Magarpatta City loop, Kharadi loop and more. Peak Pune does not organise runs; PuneRunners Club does. Running loops

Which treks are near Pune?

Peak Pune lists 20 treks and day trips with the distance from Pune and other details as published by the linked source. Peak Pune does not organise treks; groups such as Pune Trekkers and Karawaan do. Treks and day trips

What is on in Pune this week?

The radar board on the home page and the Events page show a short list, each pick linked to its source with a status. It is not an automatic feed, so some weeks it is short. Check the date, price and registration on the original listing before you go. Events

How do I plan a Pune Metro journey?

The Pune Metro guide has a journey planner that tells you which line to take, where to change and the ticket fare. It works offline after one visit. Pune Metro guide
