Peak PuneManifesto
The manifesto

Pune is not boring.
You are just looking in the wrong places.

Why Peak Pune exists, in our own words.

The best nights in this city never make the listings sites. They move through group chats and word of mouth. A gig in a basement. A food popup that exists for one Sunday. A trek that leaves while the city sleeps.

PeakPune is the radar. It exists to find the few things in Pune worth leaving the house for. It is not a directory. It will never list everything.
