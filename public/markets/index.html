Shopping areas

The main shopping grounds and what each is best for.

Old city spine

Laxmi Road

Best for: Sarees, dress material, festive wear

Pune's traditional shopping street - sarees, clothing, jewellery and festival shopping.

The old city's main commercial axis through the peths; its side lanes lead into Tulsi Baug and Raviwar Peth. Long-running saree houses here include Moolchand Mill (see standout shops).

Sahapedia: Sonya Maruti ChowkLBB Pune: saree stores
Budhwar Peth

Tulsi Baug

Best for: Budget clothing, kitchenware, puja items, accessories

A bargain bazaar grown around the Peshwa-era Ram temple complex; the name means "basil garden".

Five lanes of dense stalls; haggling is the norm. The Tulshibaug Ram Mandir, built under the Peshwas, stands inside.

Hindustan Times: Sutradhara's TalesTimes of India: Bargains galore at Tulshibaug
Shukrawar Peth

Mahatma Phule Mandai

Best for: Fresh produce, dried fruit and spice stalls around it

The 1886 Gothic market building - the city's biggest vegetable and fruit market.

Built as Reay Market; mornings are the busiest. A landmark in its own right - see the history timeline.

Indian Express: Know Your City
Old city

Raviwar Peth and Sonya Maruti Chowk

Best for: Gold and silver jewellery, old-style designs

Pune's jewellery quarter - gold and silver shops run by the same families for five or six generations.

Several jewellers here trace back over a century, including P. N. Gadgil & Sons, featured in the Indian Express "Postcards from the Past" series. Ranka Jewellers (1879) is a few lanes away on Tilak Road.

Sahapedia: Sonya Maruti ChowkIndian Express: Gadgil's golden memories
Kasba Peth

Juna Bazaar

Best for: Vintage finds and antiques

A weekly flea market for antiques, brassware, old cameras, vinyl and curios - Pune's own "chor bazaar".

Held on Wednesdays and Sundays, roughly 9 am to 5 pm, per current listings. The Times of India calls it a 250-year-old market.

LBB PuneTimes of India: Juna Bazar
Deccan Gymkhana, off FC Road

Hong Kong Lane

Best for: Budget accessories, bags, phone gear

A compact street-shopping lane for bags, footwear, phone accessories and books near Garware Bridge.

A small, dense lane; combine with the street stalls along FC Road.

LBB Pune
Shivajinagar to Deccan

Fergusson College Road

Best for: Casual clothes, accessories, college-budget shopping

Street stalls for clothing and accessories along the pavement, alongside cafes and bookshops.

Street shopping concentrates on the stalls between Deccan Gymkhana and Shivajinagar; r/pune threads routinely name FC Road for street shopping.

r/pune: Street shopping areas in Pune
Camp

MG Road and East Street

Best for: Iconic food institutions (see standout shops), general retail

The Cantonment's heritage shopping streets - Irani and Parsi-era shops, bakeries and old restaurants among the retail.

Walk East Street for Kayani Bakery and the old Camp storefronts; Fashion Street, a lane of export-surplus clothing stalls, sits off MG Road.

Standout retail

Long-running shops that anchor their areas.

Raviwar Peth - Laxmi Road · since 1832

P. N. Gadgil & Sons

Gold and silver jewellery from a house whose founding is traced to 1832.

The Gadgil family began the business in 1832 (starting in Sangli, per the company record) and its Pune shops in the Raviwar Peth-Laxmi Road quarter have served generations; the Indian Express "Postcards from the Past" profiles the house.

P. N. Gadgil & Sons: historyIndian Express: Postcards from the Past
Tilak Road · since 1879

Ranka Jewellers

Gold jewellery from a Pune family business started in 1879.

The Ranka family traces its jewellery business in Pune to 1879; the company records Shri Pukhraj Ranka re-establishing it, and seven generations have since run it. Now a multi-store group, with the Tilak Road shop the original.

Ranka Jewellers: Our Story
Deccan Gymkhana · since 1931

International Book Service

Independent English bookshop open since 1931.

Opened in 1931 beside the Deccan Gymkhana post office and among the city's oldest bookshops; the Times of India notes letters from Jawaharlal Nehru on its walls.

Indian Express: Book MarkTimes of India
Camp (Ambedkar Road)

Express Book Service

Heritage English bookshop in the Cantonment.

Hindustan Times (2017) lists Express Book Service among Pune's surviving old bookstores at over 80 years old; an exact founding year is not published, so none is claimed.

Hindustan Times: Pune bookstores
Bajirao Road

Akshardhara Book Gallery

Long-running Marathi bookshop in the old city.

A Bajirao Road institution for Marathi books; Hindustan Times (2017) lists it among the city's surviving heritage bookstores.

Hindustan Times: Pune bookstores
Laxmi Road (Budhwar Peth end)

Moolchand Mill

Multi-storey saree emporium on the old shopping street.

LBB Pune lists Moolchand Mill as one of the oldest and best-known saree shops on Laxmi Road; the founding year is not published, so none is claimed.

LBB Pune: saree stores
Deccan Gymkhana area

Kalashetram Silks

Paithani sarees - Pune's specialist name for the weave.

Named in curated Paithani guides (WedMeGood) as a Pune specialist for the weave; treated as a local-known specialist rather than a dated institution.

WedMeGood: authentic Paithani stores

Food institutions

The names Punekars actually queue at.

Camp · since 1878

Dorabjee & Sons

Parsi food, especially dhansak, in a near-unchanged old-world dining room.

Founded by Sorabjee Dorabjee in 1878 and run today by the fourth generation; the Indian Express documents a menu kept essentially the same since 1878.

Indian Express: Know Your CitySahapedia
Budhwar Peth (branches citywide) · since 1892

Kaka Halwai Sweet Centre

Classic Maharashtrian mithai - pedha, kaju katli, anjir barfi.

Founded by Kakasaheb Gadve in 1892; Loksatta and the family record trace the shop from a single Budhwar Peth unit to a citywide name.

Kaka Halwai: AboutLoksatta (Marathi)
Budhwar Peth · since 1923

Gujar Cold Drink House

Sharbat and sodas since the 1920s; later an early mastani stop.

Established in 1923 by Baburao Gujar; the Indian Express's history of mastani begins at its counter.

Indian Express: Know Your City - Mastani
FC Road, Deccan Gymkhana · since 1935

Cafe Goodluck

Irani-cafe staples - bun maska, Irani chai, caramel custard.

Set up by Hussain Ali Yakhshi in 1935; one of Pune's oldest Irani cafes, documented by Sahapedia and its own record.

Goodluck Cafe: LegacySahapedia
Laxmi Road · since 1935

Poona Guest House

Old-style Maharashtrian snacks and meals in a 1930s boarding house.

Established by Nanasaheb Sarpotdar in 1935; the Times of India notes its links with Dadasaheb Phalke's film circle.

Times of India: Tracing Pune's oldest cafes
Bajirao Road (branches worldwide) · since 1939

Chitale Bandhu Mithaiwale

Bhakarwadi - the snack that made a Pune dairy a global brand.

The Chitale family business traces to a 1939 dairy; the Bajirao Road shop made bhakarwadi a Pune signature, as The Print recounts.

Chitale Bandhu: AboutThe Print: the Chitale story
East Street, Camp · since 1955

Kayani Bakery

Shrewsbury biscuits, mawa cakes and bread - queues are part of the ritual.

Opened in August 1955 by the Kayani brothers, who had migrated from Iran; the Indian Express profiles it as a city heritage landmark.

Kayani Bakery: AboutIndian Express: Know Your City
Camp / MG Road area · since 1955

Budhani Bros Waferwala

Potato wafers fried in-house since before the packaged-chip era.

DNA traces the family shop from a kadhai and kerosene stove in 1955 to an automated line, still a city icon.

DNA: Crispy business
MG Road, Camp · since 1965

Marz-o-rin

Sandwiches, cold coffee and plum cake in a heritage building.

Sheriar Sheriyarji opened it on 11 February 1965 with four items; the family tells the story on its site, and the Indian Express profiles the founders.

Marz-o-rin: Our StoryIndian Express
Sadashiv Peth (original), branches citywide · since 1967

Sujata Mastani

Mastani - Pune's thick milkshake-dessert; the rose flavour came first.

Sharadrao Kondhalkar started the shop in 1967; India Today records the original 80-paise rose mastani. The drink's name nods to Mastani of Peshwa-era fame, as the Indian Express explains.

India TodaySujata Mastani: About
Sangamvadi, near Ruby Hall Clinic · since 1978

Vohuman Cafe

Cheese omelette, bun maska and Irani chai.

Hormuz Irani opened it on 15 August 1978; Sahapedia and the Indian Express both record the story.

SahapediaIndian Express: Pune on my plate
Old city

Laxminarayan Chivda

The city's signature chivda - poha, peanuts, copra and raisins.

Founded by hawker-turned-merchant Laxminarayan Data; published accounts place the founding in the early 1900s but differ on the exact year, so no "since" date is claimed here.
