Koregaon Park, Pune

About Koregaon Park

Koregaon Park is a leafy, mostly residential neighbourhood in the east of Pune, with a busy strip of restaurants, cafes, bars and galleries. Its lanes are lined with older bungalows and large trees, which is why people come here for morning and evening walks as well as meals. Two main roads, North Main Road and South Main Road, run roughly parallel, joined by numbered lanes. Lanes 6 and 7 are the ones best known for pubs and food places. The Osho International Meditation Resort and the Osho Teerth Park, on Dhunjibhoy Road between lanes 2 and 3, are also here. Source: Wikipedia, Koregaon Park.

Getting to Koregaon Park

Bund Garden station on Pune Metro Line 2 (Aqua) is on Bund Garden Road, at the western edge of Koregaon Park, on the same side of the Mula-Mutha river as the neighbourhood. The Bund Garden bridge is what crosses the river, to Yerawada. Routing on OpenStreetMap data (routing.openstreetmap.de, walking profile) gives about 1.9 km, around 25 minutes on foot, from the station to Osho Teerth Park. Pavements and crossings vary, so check the route on a map before you set out.

From Osho Teerth Park, the same routing service gives about 3.3 km by road to Pune Junction railway station and about 8.0 km by road to Pune airport. These are road distances without traffic. For fares, line changes and ticketing, see the Pune Metro guide.

Running here

The shaded lanes make Koregaon Park a regular morning running area. See the Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar loop for a route, and Pune running meetups for groups that run here.

The busy centre: North Main Road and Lanes 5 to 7

Travel and Leisure Asia describes Koregaon Park as sandwiched between North Main Road and South Main Road, with the lanes joining them. Most lanes are leafy and residential, while Lanes 6 and 7 are where the restaurants, bars and cafes cluster. It also notes street food kiosks on Lane 6, and Lane 1, near the Osho International Meditation Resort, has Sante Spa Cuisine.

Places that guides place on this strip include Malaka Spice (Lane 5), Arthur's Theme (Lane 6), Effingut and Pimlico. On the map, this is the dense cluster of pins around North Main Road and the lanes south of it. The restaurant, cafe and bar lists below include every listed place.

Sources: Travel and Leisure Asia, 11 Oct 2024, Wikipedia, Koregaon Park. Read 23 November 2026.

Quieter corners

Away from the main strip, the same Travel and Leisure Asia guide suggests simply walking the lanes and bylanes to see the older bungalows. Parks and galleries on the page sit mostly off the busiest stretch: Osho Teerth Park between Lane 2 and Lane 3, Monalisa Kalagram at the end of South Main Road, and Vida Heydari Contemporary on Lane 8. LBB describes Lane 8 as quiet and green.

The map also pins Damodar Wagaskar Garden, Meera Nagar Park and Shahu Modak Udyan, listed in OpenStreetMap as parks.

Sources: Travel and Leisure Asia, 11 Oct 2024, LBB on VHC, Lane 8. Read 23 November 2026.

Marigold Complex, Kalyani Nagar

The Arbour, in Marigold Complex (Marisoft IT Park), is a cluster of restaurants and bars listed under Kalyani Nagar. It is outside the Koregaon Park boundary used on this page's map, so the places below are not pinned.

The Nines is a restaurant and bar on the 2nd floor of The Arbour. People & Co is a lounge bar in the same complex, and kai.ros is listed there too. Check each listing for current hours, prices and offers.

Sources: The Nines on District, People And Co on District, People and Co. site, kai.ros on District. Read 23 November 2026.

Worth knowing

Described from each place's own site or a named publication. Peak Pune has not visited or ranked them.

Park · D. H. Dhunjibhoy Road, between Lane 2 and Lane 3

Osho Teerth Park

A 12-acre garden on what was garbage dumps and eroded land with an open sewer running through it. The Osho International Foundation's Shunyo Foundation ran the restoration from May 1990 to September 1991, according to The Indian Express. It added drainage lines, four ponds in a series, paths and bridges, and a plant nursery.

Entry is free. The park is open only at set morning and evening times, so check the current hours before you go. The Indian Express puts weekend visits at around 2,500 a day.

Sources: The Indian Express, 14 Jun 2025, OpenStreetMap way 28217451. Read 23 November 2026.

Landmark · Koregaon Park (shown on the map as Osho Ashram, the name OpenStreetMap uses)

Osho International Meditation Resort

A residential and day-visit meditation campus. Per its own FAQ, there are no tours and you can only enter the campus as a participant, registered at the Welcome Center. Its Osho Multimedia Gallery can be visited from the road near the main gate, 9 am to 1 pm and 2 pm to 4 pm.

The FAQ lists a one-day entry pass at 1,100 INR for resident Indian nationals and 2,100 INR for international visitors, plus a one-time 100 INR gate pass for first-time visitors. Maroon robes are worn in the daytime and white robes for the evening meeting. It describes itself as an environment for adults, without facilities for children and minors. Prices and rules can change, so confirm on the resort's site.

Sources: Osho Meditation Resort FAQ, Welcome Center. Read 23 November 2026.

Art gallery · Pingale Farms, end of South Main Road

Monalisa Kalagram

An art and culture space with rotating exhibitions. Its site lists 11 am to 7 pm, closed Tuesdays, with an exhibition running 17 October to 9 November 2025. That date is old, so check what is on now.

Sources: Monalisa Kalagram. Read 23 November 2026.

Art gallery · Marvel Alaina, Lane 8

Vida Heydari Contemporary (VHC)

A contemporary art gallery. Its site lists 10:00 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Saturday, and gives the address as Marvel Alaina, Lane 8, Koregaon Park.

Sources: VHC. Read 23 November 2026.

Restaurant · Lane 5, opposite Oxford Properties

Malaka Spice

A South-East Asian restaurant. The brand says it works farm-to-table through its own Cherish Farm. Its Koregaon Park outlet page lists the opening hours, which you should check before going.

Sources: Malaka Spice, Koregaon Park outlet. Read 23 November 2026.

Restaurant · Vrindavan Apartment, Lane 6

Arthur's Theme

A European-style restaurant that its site says opened in Koregaon Park in 1997. The site lists 11:30 am to 11:30 pm daily and publishes menu prices.

Sources: Arthur's Theme. Read 23 November 2026.

Bar · Ground Floor, Gera Legend, North Main Road

Hidden Place

A bar with bites, continental dishes and family-style Indian meals on the menu, according to its own site, which lists opening hours of 12 PM to 12:30 AM. LBB describes it as having a discreet entrance and a compact, cosy space. This is a bar called Hidden Place, which is not the same as an area of hidden spots.

Sources: Hidden Place, LBB, bars in Koregaon Park. Read 23 November 2026.

Where this list comes from

Names, cuisines, street names and opening hours come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), read on 23 November 2026. Only places inside the area OpenStreetMap maps as Koregaon Park are included, so places just outside it, such as Kalyani Nagar, are not. OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers. Peak Pune did not visit or rank these places, so a venue may have closed, moved or changed its hours. Check with the venue before you go. Each name links to its OpenStreetMap entry, where anyone can fix it. The map itself is drawn from OpenStreetMap data by OpenMapTiles vector tiles served by OpenFreeMap.
