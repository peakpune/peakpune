About

What Peak Pune is,
and how it is checked.

Peak Pune is a Pune-based discovery site that gathers source-checked places, events, running loops, treks and meetups in one place. We do not organise runs or treks; we point to the people who do.

Why does Peak Pune exist?

Good plans in Pune are scattered across venue websites, club pages and ticketing platforms. Peak Pune puts them in one scroll and sends you back to the original page for dates, prices and bookings. The site is free, shows no ads and has no accounts.

Who runs it?

Peak Pune is an open-source project. Its code, its data and the record of what was published, corrected or withheld are public at github.com/peakpune/peakpune, and anyone can propose a change there. The owner reviews every change before it goes live.

How are listings checked?

  • Every listing links to its source. Addresses, timings and prices are read from the venue's or organiser's own page, and each page shows the date it was checked.
  • Unknown stays unknown. If a source does not state a detail, we leave it out. A missing price is never shown as free.
  • Nothing is published by default. A listing appears only after its entry is approved in the public audit file.
  • A person reads every submitted plan before it shows on the Events page.
  • Platform listings are labelled. Plans read from a ticketing platform are marked as source-listed, not organiser-confirmed.
  • Old checks are flagged. When a listing's check date gets old, the page says so and asks you to confirm with the venue.

Details change. Always confirm on the linked page before you travel or pay.

Who organises the runs and treks?

Not us. Running loops come from PuneRunners Club, trek guides from Pune Trekkers, and trips from Karawaan. See Powered by for what each group does and where to join.

How do I report a mistake or get in touch?

Open an issue at github.com/peakpune/peakpune/issues with the page and the correct source. For a security problem, use the private "Report a vulnerability" option on the repository's Security tab instead of a public issue. You can also reach the community through the WhatsApp links at the top of every page.
