# Listing audit: 13 November 2026

The visible preview label has been removed. That does not enable search indexing, submissions, uploads or a booking service.

## Publication rule

Only IDs explicitly marked `publish` in `data/publication-audit.json` are built into public listing pages. A new or unreviewed row is withheld by default. Corrections are applied there as explicit overrides; original batch rows remain available for review and in git history. Reopening a withheld record needs a new source check and an explicit publication decision.

Source-backed means a cited page supports the identity and displayed claims, not that Peak Pune has visited a venue, run a route, confirmed an event with its organiser, checked live ticket inventory or booked anything.

## Results

- 28 place listings retained. Current primary venue pages or district tourism pages support their identity. Unknown details stay unknown.
- 8 mapped running routes and 20 trek/day-trip guides retained. Route distances and guide facts match their cited sources. Mapped routes are not field-tested; trail access, weather and safety need checking before going.
- 9 additional source-listed event/schedule cards retained. Pune International Marathon is now linked to its organiser's race information page. Community race guides are not presented as organiser confirmation.
- 7 communities retained with public joining information and current dated source activity.
- 12 generated Moments captions removed. They were editorial writing, not submissions or witnessed moments. The archive teaser has been removed too. No genuine submitted moments are published yet.
- 6 community cards withheld because current dated activity was not confirmed. These communities are not being called fake.
- The Shaniwar Wada light-and-music-show card is withheld: the tourism page has no dated/current show schedule. The real monument remains listed without a daily-show promise.
- The Geminid camp stays withheld: it is not on the current rendered trip list. Its proposed date and IUCAA researcher claim remain unverified.
- 19 map-only place records remain unpublished, as before this audit.
- All 43 original District URLs were opened: titles, dates/recurrences and Pune venues match the snapshot. The original 24-hour expiry has not been extended. These are platform listings, not independent organiser confirmation. Prices remain subject to change. A rounded brunch price was corrected to INR 2771.82 and the explicit free event is represented as `Free` rather than `INR Free`. The current cards do not advertise prices.

## Detail corrections

- Removed Monalisa Kalagram hours copied from an October-November 2025 exhibition. The gallery is real; its current exhibition and visiting hours are not confirmed.
- Removed Pagdandi and Chitale shop hours supported only by map data.
- Removed conflicting Malaka Spice Koregaon Park hours and unconfirmed current Toit/Wadeshwar hours.
- Removed Marz-O-Rin example prices that were not confirmed by the current source page.
- Removed the unsupported weekly-run wording from PuneRunners. Its own page says it is not a weekly run club; members make their own plans.
- Corrected Khadakwasla's distance from Pune to the district page's approximate 20 km, rather than the row's 15 km.
- Corrected the museum closure-day summary and retained the exact published holiday list.
- Checked all 19 existing place-map objects against the current OpenStreetMap object API. Their positions agree at venue scale. The unnamed Akshardhara node is no longer shown as a venue pin.
